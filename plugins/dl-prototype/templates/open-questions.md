# Open questions

The single register for the project, unless the constitution's "Open questions register" section names another master. Every `?` in a spec has a row here, and cites its ID on the same line.

Blocking is against the current milestone: Yes, No, or `?` until triaged. A feature with an open row that is Yes or `?` cannot be marked Ready.

One table per feature under Open, newest row at the top. IDs are never reused; imported rows keep their original IDs.
